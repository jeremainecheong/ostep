The whole book, as sixteen interactive lessons

Built from the text of all 57 chapters plus the VMM appendix — every "crux", every summary, every number in the figures was extracted from the chapter PDFs, not recalled. Each lesson is short, visual, and ends in retrieval practice. Do them in order; the book's pieces build on each other.

Course map

How to use this course

1. One lesson per sitting

Each takes 25–45 minutes: read, play with the simulator until you can predict its output, flip the cards, then do the quiz from memory. Check off the objectives at the top only when you could explain them to someone else.

2. Then read the chapter

Every lesson links its primary source — the free chapter PDFs at ostep.org. The lesson gives you the map; the chapter has the jokes, the asides, and the homework simulators (ostep-homework) which are the best practice there is.

3. Space it out

Long-term retention comes from re-testing later, not from feeling fluent now. Redo a lesson's quiz two days later and again after a week; the cheat sheets are for the fast pass, the glossary for vocabulary. Ask me in the chat for mixed quizzes across lessons.

What the book covers that this course does too
